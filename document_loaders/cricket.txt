Cricket is a popular bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of 11 players each. 
It is governed globally by the International Cricket Council (ICC) and stands as the second most-watched sport in the world.

The fundamental goal is to score more runs than the opposing team. 
One team bats to score runs, while the other fields and bowls to restrict runs and dismiss the batters.

The Pitch & Wickets: The action centers on a 22-yard rectangular strip (the pitch) in the middle of a large oval field. 
At each end of the pitch sits a "wicket"—three wooden stumps holding two small wooden pieces called bails on top.

Batting in Pairs: The batting team always has two players on the pitch simultaneously (the striker and non-striker).

Overs: A bowler delivers a set of 6 legal balls, which constitutes an "over". After an over is complete, 
a different bowler delivers from the opposite end of the pitch.

When a batter is dismissed, they leave the field and are replaced by the next teammate. 
Once 10 out of the 11 batters are dismissed, the teams switch roles.
